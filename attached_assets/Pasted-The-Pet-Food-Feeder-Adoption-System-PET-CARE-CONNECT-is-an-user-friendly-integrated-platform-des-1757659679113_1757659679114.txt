The Pet Food Feeder & Adoption System (PET CARE CONNECT) is an user friendly integrated platform designed to streamline pet care, adoption, and food management. The system is built to serve Pet Owners, Adoption Agencies, and Pet Food Suppliers in a seamless and user-friendly environment. Have a support of AI chatbot for knowing about pets and there caring methods only for pet owners. For Pet Owners, the system provides features such as creating and managing pet profiles including pet photo, setting up personalized feeding schedules and alert on feeding time via mail or text message, tracking food consumption, and applying for pet adoption, view and purchase pet foods and track its delivery. The pet owners can know whether their adoption application approved or not. Pet owners can also search and view available pets for adoption and monitor their adoption status in real time. Additionally, they can post feedback on adoption experiences and pet food suppliers. Adoption Agencies (Admins) can manage pet adoption applications, approve or reject requests, and maintain updated pet listings including pet profiles with its photo (add, edit, delete). Admins also oversee user feedback, ensuring quality service for both pets and owners. They are responsible for reviewing all adoption requests and ensuring the correct placement of pets in loving homes. Pet Food Suppliers/Owners can manage subscriptions for pet food, track orders and sell products to the pet owners, and update food inventories with its photo (add, edit, delete). The system supports flexible subscription models and timely deliveries, ensuring that pets receive the correct amount of food as per their needs. Overall, this platform enhances the experience of pet care by integrating pet food feeding, adoption processes, and community interaction into a unified system. By using this system, pet owners can ensure timely feeding, connect with adoption services, and contribute to a healthier, happier environment for pets. 

Frontend – html,css,bootstrap
Backend – python flask
